📝 How to Use

1

Image Gallery

Click "View" on any image or hover to open the lightbox. Use filter buttons to sort by category. Navigate with arrow keys or prev/next buttons.

2

Calculator

Click buttons or use your keyboard to enter numbers and operations. Press Enter or click "=" to calculate. Use Backspace to delete the last digit.

3

Music Player

Click the play button to start. Use next/previous buttons to navigate the playlist. Adjust volume with the slider. Click the playlist icon to view all songs.
