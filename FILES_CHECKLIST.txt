🎯 HOW TO OPEN & TEST
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Step 1: Open the Landing Page
  → Double-click: frontend/index.html
  → Or drag to browser: frontend/index.html

Step 2: Explore Projects
  → Click "Open Gallery" → View and filter images
  → Click "Open Calculator" → Perform calculations
  → Click "Open Player" → Play sample music

Step 3: Test Features
  Image Gallery:
    • Click filter buttons to sort by category
    • Click "View" to open lightbox
    • Use arrow keys to navigate
    • Press ESC to close
  
  Calculator:
    • Click number buttons or use keyboard (0-9)
    • Click operators or use keyboard (+, -, *, /)
    • Click = or press Enter to calculate
    • Use Backspace to delete
  
  Music Player:
    • Click play button to start
    • Use next/previous buttons to navigate
    • Adjust volume with slider
    • Click playlist button to view songs
